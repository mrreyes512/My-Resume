# Year-End Review

## Overview

In my first year as a contractor at Cigna, I was entrusted with key challenges within GNS:

1. Enhance communication within GNS's Automation Community.
2. Identify ways for NDO to provide value through guidance and collaboration.

As I took the lead in GNS's Automation Community, I quickly identified opportunities for improvement and addressed the root causes of the initial concerns outlined throughout 2024.

---

## Key Accomplishments

### Automation Community

- Conducted **52 bi-weekly meetings**, fostering a culture of collaboration and trust across teams.
- Cataloged scripts with the **GNS Script Library** and presented them to leadership.
- Established regular 1:1s with Team Leaders and conducted **38 technical 1:1s** to address challenges and provide direct support.

### Ansible Automation Platform (AAP)

- Identified Itential as too complex for development, which had led to decreased participation and engagement.
- Designed a **Slido survey** to empower individual contributors in selecting the preferred Automation Platform between Itential and AAP.
- Achieved **participation from 73 members**, resulting in the successful migration to AAP.

### Training and Procedural Discovery

- **Produced 19 documents** in the GNS Guides Training Repository published on GitHub:
  - These resources not only provided training materials but also required memberships to Global Groups, enabling seamless collaboration through Git.
- Discovered and documented key procedures to standardize GNS operations:
  - **Variable Encryption with Ansible Vault** for local development and migration to AAP for production-ready scripts.
  - **Ansible Collection Distribution** between GNS teams.
  - **Dynamic Inventory Integration** with Nautobot.
